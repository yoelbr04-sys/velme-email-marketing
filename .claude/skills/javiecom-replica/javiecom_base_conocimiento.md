## 1. Movimientos de cámara

| # | Nombre | Línea exacta |
|---|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Plano fijo | Static camera, locked-off shot, no camera movement |
| 2 | Zoom lento a la cara | Slow, smooth zoom in toward the face |
| 3 | Zoom rápido al hablar | Quick punch-in zoom toward the face as they start speaking |
| 4 | En mano sutil | Subtle handheld camera with gentle natural micro-shake, like a phone filmed in the hand |
| 5 | Push-in suave | Smooth, slow push-in toward the subject |
| 6 | Retroceso que revela | Camera slowly pulls back to reveal more of the scene |
| 7 | Órbita lenta | Slow orbit around the subject, keeping them centered in frame |
| 8 | Seguimiento | Camera smoothly follows the subject as they move |
| 9 | El más usado / recomendado | Subtle handheld camera with gentle natural micro-shake, like a phone filmed in the hand |

Recomendados por seguridad y naturalidad: plano fijo (1) y zoom lento a la cara (2). Para producto en primera persona (POV): en mano sutil (4) o push-in suave (5).

- gives a thumbs up
- claps once
- tilts their head curiously
- adjusts their glasses
- tucks hair behind their ear
- taps their chin thoughtfully
- snaps their fingers

Estructuras:
- Antes de hablar: `The person [acción], then looks at the camera and says: "[guión]"`
- Mientras/después: `The person says: "[guión]" [acción]`

## 3. Skincare

`While speaking, she gradually blends the visible cream into the [sitio] until it is smoothly absorbed into the skin. The amount of visible cream continuously decreases throughout the clip, leaving no residue, streaks, or buildup.`

`[sitio]` solo puede ser: under-eye area, cheeks, forehead, neck.

## 7. Retoques de imagen

| Retoque | Línea |
|---------|-------|
| Cambiar avatar | Replace the person in the reference image with [descripción], keeping the same pose, framing, lighting and background. |
| Cambiar ropa | Change only the clothing to [descripción]. Keep the person, pose, background and lighting exactly the same. |
| Cambiar fondo | Change only the background to [descripción]. Keep the person, pose, clothing and lighting exactly the same. |
| Acercar plano | Re-frame the image as a closer shot of [sujeto], keeping the same person, clothing, lighting and background. |
| Reemplazar persona | Replace the person with a different person, keeping the same pose, framing, clothing, lighting and background. |
| Voltear imagen | Mirror the image horizontally, keeping everything else identical. |
